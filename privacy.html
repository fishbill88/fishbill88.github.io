Privacy policy

TaskTopFight does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry. Nothing the game reads on your machine ever leaves it.

Last updated 4 September 2026.

What the game reads, and why

The fight escalates the longer you leave the keyboard alone, so the game has to know whether you are at the desk. To do that it reads two things locally:

  • Whether input is happening. The game watches for mouse and keyboard activity in order to measure how many seconds have passed since the last one. It records that input occurred, never what was typed or clicked. It is not a keylogger and it captures no keystroke content of any kind.
  • Whether sound is playing, and whether an app is full-screen. This is how a film holds the idle clock instead of resetting it. The game reads audio peak levels and the system's full-screen notification state. It does not read what is playing, which application is playing it, or any audio content.

Both checks run entirely on your computer, produce nothing but a number of seconds, and are never sent anywhere. You can turn the audio check off under Idle while watching in the ⚙ panel.

Your save file

Your progress — hero, level, gear, skill tree and settings — is written to a file in the game's own data folder on your PC. If you have Steam Cloud enabled, Steam copies that file to your Steam account so the same hero appears on another PC. That transfer is performed by Steam under Valve's privacy policy; the save holds game progress only, and we never receive a copy.

Ctrl+Alt+R deletes the save, including the on-disk copy.

What we receive

Nothing automatic. The only information we ever hold is what you choose to put in an email to us, and we keep that only as long as it takes to answer you.

Because you buy the game through Steam, Valve — not us — handles your purchase, payment details and account. We never see them. Valve provides developers with aggregate, anonymous sales and wishlist statistics that cannot identify an individual player.

Updates

Game updates are delivered by Steam. The game does not contact any server of ours to check for them, and it has no update mechanism of its own.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you the right to see, correct or delete personal data held about you. In our case there is none to show, correct or delete — but if you have emailed us and want that correspondence erased, write to tasktopfight@gmail.com and it will be.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the update notes on Steam. The date at the top always reflects the current text.
