A fight on top of your taskbar.

TaskTopFight is a 64-pixel-tall combat strip pinned to the bottom edge of your screen. Your hero holds the centre, enemies run in from both sides, and the whole thing carries on while you work. Stop typing for ten seconds and they start dropping in from the sky.

What it actually is

It stays out of the way

The window is click-through and never takes focus, so it cannot steal a keystroke from whatever you were really doing.

Idle is the mechanic

The longer you leave the keyboard alone, the more the fight escalates — ground spawns give way to enemies falling in from the top of the screen.

Twelve classes

Each with a four-tier skill set, its own animation vocabulary and a distinct silhouette at 64 pixels.

Gear, sets and a forge

Drops roll by rarity and band. Armour sets pay out on completion, and the forge turns the rest into something you will actually equip.

A skill tree with a refund button

Respec whenever. Nothing about a build should be a trap you cannot get back out of.

It knows when you are watching something

Full-screen video holds the idle clock rather than resetting it, so a film neither buries the strip nor banks you free progress.

System requirements

OSWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit
ProcessorAny x64 CPU from the last decade
Memory4 GB RAM
Storage250 MB
Display1280×720 or wider
Not supportedmacOS, Linux, Steam Deck, controllers
TaskTopFight is a desktop-only game by design — it lives on the Windows taskbar and expects a mouse and a keyboard. It does not run in Big Picture mode and is not playable on Steam Deck.
