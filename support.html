Support

Email tasktopfight@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a person, usually within a few days. Include your Windows version and what the strip was doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

It is sitting on top of my taskbar. Is that a bug?
No — that is the whole game. TaskTopFight is a thin strip that lives on the bottom edge of your screen, above the taskbar, permanently. It is click-through, so it cannot intercept a click meant for something behind it.
How do I hide it without quitting?
Open the ⚙ panel on the strip and hide the playable area. The game keeps running and the setting survives a restart, so it comes back hidden if that is how you left it.
Where is my save?
On disk, in the app's own data folder, and synced by Steam Cloud so a second PC picks up the same hero. It is deliberately kept out of the game folder, which means verifying or reinstalling the game will not touch it.
How do I start over?
Ctrl+Alt+R wipes the save and starts a fresh world. This is not reversible and it clears the on-disk copy too, so it will not quietly come back on the next launch.
Does it slow my machine down?
It should not. If you are seeing fan noise or a battery hit, tell us what else was on screen at the time — the idle and video watchers are the first place we look.
My hero came back naked / at the wrong level.
Email us before you play any further. The save has an on-disk copy that we can usually talk you through restoring, but continuing to play can overwrite it.
How do I uninstall it?
Right-click TaskTopFight in your Steam library → Manage → Uninstall. Your save is left behind on purpose, so reinstalling picks up where you left off. Use Ctrl+Alt+R first if you want it gone as well.

Known issues

  • The Steam overlay does not open over the strip. Shift-Tab, Steam screenshots and achievement pop-ups will not appear on top of the game. Achievements still unlock normally and show up in your profile — you just will not see the notification. This is a limitation of how the game renders and is not something a setting can fix.
  • Windows only. There is no macOS, Linux or Steam Deck build, and none is planned.
  • No controller support. The game expects a mouse and a keyboard.

Reporting a bug

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • the version, from the footer of the ⚙ panel
  • your Windows version and how many monitors you use
  • what was on screen when it happened
  • a screenshot, if it is something you can see

Send it to tasktopfight@gmail.com. If you would rather file it in public — or want to check whether someone has already hit the same thing — the tracker is on GitHub.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Steam, not by us — request one through Steam Support, normally within 14 days of purchase and under two hours of playtime. We do not need to approve it and we are not told why you asked.
