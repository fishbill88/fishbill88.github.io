Short description

A 64-pixel combat strip that lives on your Windows taskbar. Your hero fights while you work — and when you stop, the sky starts dropping enemies on it.

Long description

TaskTopFight is an idle action game that occupies a thin strip along the bottom edge of your screen and never asks for your attention. Your hero holds the centre while enemies run in from either side. The window is click-through and refuses focus, so the fight is something you glance at rather than something you have to manage.

Walking away is the mechanic. The longer the keyboard sits untouched, the harder the strip works: ground spawns give way to enemies falling in from the top of the screen, and the fight that greets you when you come back is a record of how long you were gone. The game can also tell when you are watching something full-screen, and holds the clock rather than resetting it, so a film neither buries the strip nor hands you free progress.

Underneath the strip there is a full progression game: twelve classes with four-tier skill sets and their own animation vocabularies, gear that rolls by rarity and band, armour sets that pay out on completion, a forge for everything else, and a skill tree you can respec at any time.

Features

  • Lives on the taskbar; click-through and never steals focus
  • Idle escalation, with sky drop-ins when you are away from the desk
  • Twelve classes, each with a distinct silhouette at 64 pixels
  • Gear, armour sets and a forge
  • A freely refundable skill tree
  • No accounts, no telemetry, no data collection of any kind
